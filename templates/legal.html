Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

GuideToAnime is a small anime guide and watch tracker. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site handles.

What we store

  • Account: the username you choose and your password, which is stored only as a secure hash (we cannot read it). We do not ask for your email address, name or phone number.
  • Your activity: the list of anime you tick as watched, and the avatar you pick (if you have Premium).
  • Account dates: when you joined and when you last logged in.
  • Tokens: your token balance, when your Premium period ends, and a history of token changes (such as spending tokens on Premium).
  • Pictures you upload: only if you are a Premium user and add your own avatars.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics on your activity.

Other websites your browser contacts

Anime posters and character pictures are loaded from the image servers of AniList, MyAnimeList and Kitsu, and the characters row may ask AniList or MyAnimeList (Jikan) for a picture. When your browser loads these, those services can see your IP address, as with any website. Please see their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • You can download your watched list from the account menu (Export).
  • You can delete your account at any time from the account menu. This removes your username, password hash, watched list and the avatars you uploaded.

Security

Passwords are hashed and sessions are protected, but no website is perfectly secure. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else. Because we have no email on file, we cannot reset a forgotten password by email.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: contact page.
